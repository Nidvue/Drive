# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Nidvue Drive to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people. Review the change, not the author.
- Assume competence: ask why before concluding it is wrong.
- Accept that "this needs no change" is a valid review outcome.
- Keep disagreement about the work; take it to the maintainers, not to Twitter.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, sustained disruption, publishing
private information, or sexual attention of any kind.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any
project space. Report to the maintainers via the address in `SECURITY.md`.
Maintainers may warn, edit or remove contributions, or ban participation.
Enforcement decisions are recorded and appealable once.
